Activation Guide:

Open Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or another Office application.
Select Sign In or the activation option.
Sign in with the Microsoft account associated with your subscription or license.
Follow the on-screen instructions.
Confirm that Office is activated.

If activation fails, check your account, subscription/license status, internet connection, and Office installation.
